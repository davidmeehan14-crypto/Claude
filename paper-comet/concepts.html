Six directions for the comet bot

Same palette, same lighting, same tails. Only the head changes. Drag any one to turn it. Use the toggles to judge the pure shape and how each reads at chat-button size.

The small circle in each card is a live 56px render.

My read

A mascot has to pass two tests: you can draw it from memory, and it still reads at 56px. The strongest here on both counts are C Gem (the most "paper" and the most distinctive outline) and B Visor (the most "bot", and the helmet rim gives a clean recognisable shape). A Teardrop is the most comet-like but closest to a generic blob. D Dart is the most on-brand for "paper" but the long nose hides the face. E Pod is clean but reads as a capsule or a pill. F Hood is cute and soft, though the face has nothing to anchor it.

  1. Pick one direction, or a blend (for example C's gem head with B's visor slot).
  2. I then design the face, antenna and tails properly for that head, with real crease lines, baked shading and a true glow.
  3. The behaviour system from the current demo plugs into whichever head wins.
